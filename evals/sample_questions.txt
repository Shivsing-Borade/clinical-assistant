============================================================
 CLINICAL ANSWER 
============================================================

For fever over 100°F, paracetamol is recommended at 10 mg/kg/dose, administered no more frequently than every six hours, up to a maximum adult dose of 4 gm/day. Aspirin and NSAIDs should be avoided.
